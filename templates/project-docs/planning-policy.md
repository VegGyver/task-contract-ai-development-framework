# Planning Policy

## Organizational execution profile

Profile: `unspecified`

Allowed values: `unspecified`, `single-developer`, `team`, `multi-team`.

`unspecified` is the default when this role is absent. It keeps planning team-capable without inventing people, owners or artificial serialization.

## Decomposition mode

Mode: `standard`

Allowed values: `standard`, `custom`.

## Custom decomposition policy

None.

When Mode is `custom`, replace `None.` with the approved project/team-specific rules for task grouping, decomposition heuristics, ownership conventions or examples.

A custom policy may refine planning organization but may not waive TCAF invariants or the currently active shared-contract/API-boundary rule.
